About Villa

Websites should do more than exist.

I started out customizing themes for friends' small businesses and kept going because I liked the moment a client saw their site load for the first time and finally felt proud to share it. Since then I've worked with restaurants, law firms, travel agencies, and early-stage founders — always as a one-person team, always hands-on from the first sketch to the final line of code.

I care about three things on every project: it has to load fast, it has to be simple for you to update without me, and it has to look like nothing else in your industry. Most of my clients come from referrals, which tells me the work speaks for itself.

i

Built around your business

No recycled templates — every layout is built from your content, your goals and your customers, not a generic starting point.

ii

Fast by default

Hand-coded front ends load in a fraction of the time of typical page-builder sites, which matters for both visitors and search rankings.

iii

You're never locked out

You get clear documentation and a simple way to update your own content — no waiting on me for small text changes.
